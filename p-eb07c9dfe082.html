Privacy

What you write stays yours.

keep. holds some of your most honest sentences. It treats them that way.

No ads. No tracking.

keep. doesn’t show ads and doesn’t follow you around other apps or websites.

Your words never become analytics.

Usage analytics are off unless you turn them on — and even then, the text of your Keeps, reflections and check-ins is never sent.
