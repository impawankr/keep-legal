A small ritual, every day

No streak pressure, no feed, no noise. Four quiet steps, in the same order each time, so starting gets easier than putting it off.

  1. first

    Check in

    Pick how you’re feeling, in a tap. A line about why, if you like.

  2. then

    Write a Keep

    One thing you’ll do now, filed under a promise you’re keeping to yourself.

  3. then

    Focus

    Hand it to Panda and put the phone down. A calm paper timer counts the minutes.

  4. last

    Reflect

    What got done, what’s left. Panda reads it back to you, kindly.

Watch it add up

Every Keep is a step. Over weeks they become a picture of when you focus best, where your time really goes, and how your days have felt.
